SC STRENGTH FILTER

For each of SC1, SC2 and SC3: Total Wick / Body × 100 ≤ current editable limit. One weak SC invalidates the entire setup. Doji/zero-body SC is invalid.

LONG · G G G R → TC

SC1, SC2, SC3 are Green; RC is Red. Highest High across all four candles is the breakout level. TC must cross above it.

SHORT · R R R G → TC

SC1, SC2, SC3 are Red; RC is Green. Lowest Low across all four candles is the breakdown level. TC must cross below it.

ENTRY-CANDLE RULE

TC can trigger the trade. At TC close, a close beyond SL is Systematic SL; otherwise SL activates from the next candle. Later candles resolve normal SL/target.

FAILED SETUP STUDY

Weak-SC failures and strong-setup/no-cross failures are recorded for study only. No later candle can revive a failed setup.

OHLC LIMITATION

OHLC proves a level was crossed but not the exact tick. Entry is recorded at the theoretical level. Same-candle SL + target is Ambiguous.
